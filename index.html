Who We Are

Built on Compassion,
Driven by Purpose

Founded on September 23, 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla, InAmigos Foundation is dedicated to creating lasting social impact through a network of dedicated professionals and volunteers spread across multiple states of India.

  • Empowering underprivileged children with quality education and digital literacy
  • Providing nutritious meals and essential clothing to those in need
  • Training women with vocational skills for financial independence
  • Promoting environmental sustainability through tree-planting drives
  • Offering skill development and internship programs to 30,000+ youth
  • Working closely with government welfare departments during relief operations

What We Do

Our Flagship Projects

Six transformative initiatives addressing the most critical needs of underserved communities across India.

🍱
Food & Clothing
Project SEVA

Tackling hunger and inadequate clothing among impoverished communities. Volunteers distribute nutritious meals and essential clothing to the homeless every week.

50,000+Meals & Clothing Distributed
📚
Education
Project Bachpanshala

Empowering underprivileged children in rural areas with quality education, digital literacy, and life skills — nurturing dreams and shaping futures.

Rural IndiaEducation in Underserved Areas
🪁
Women Empowerment
Project Udaan

A flagship women empowerment initiative providing vocational training, mentorship, and micro-enterprise support. Scaling to 10 states to reach 20,000+ women by 2026.

900+Women Trained · 3 States
🌳
Environment
Project Prakriti

Organizing tree-planting drives, clean-up campaigns, and conservation workshops. Involving local communities to build environmental responsibility for future generations.

🌱Trees Planted · Clean India Mission
💼
Skill Development
Project VIKAS

Facilitating employment through internships in data operations, finance, research, content writing, digital marketing, and social work fields.

30,000+Interns Trained in 4 Years
🐾
Animal Welfare
Project JEEV

Dedicated to the care of stray and abandoned animals — providing food, medical aid, and shelter support to ensure their dignity and wellbeing.

🐶Animals Fed & Cared For
